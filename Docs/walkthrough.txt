*** SPOILER ALERT ***
---------------------
The following contains a walkthrough guide for the game:

- Click to dismiss the letter.

- Click on the table with the microscope.

- Click and drag the left-most petri dish under the microscope. This is the control.

- Click on the microscope to look through it.

- Click on the view to investigate the culture. This will trigger some dialogue. The character will say it reminds them of a fungus.

- Click away from the microscope view to exit the microscope.

- Replace the fungal specimen under the petri dish with the middle one.

- Go into the microscope view and examine the new petri dish. The character will say it looks as if the fungal cells are phototactic.

- Go back to the main room view and click on the books on the table.

- Click on the search bar to type "conidia" into the search bar. A page will appear.

- Hit the return key or click to close the page and type "dormant" into the search bar.

- Close the page and type "thermophilic".

- Close the thermophilic page and return to the lab view on the other table.

- Look at the control petri dish under the microscope. The character will say it could be thermophilic.

- Go back to the room view and click on the thermostat on the wall.

- Increase its temperature to 25 degrees and change the mode to heating.

- Go back to the microscope and observe the control petri dish again. The character will confirm that they are indeed thermophilic.

- Place another petri dish under the microscope.

- Go into the attic by clicking on the hatch on the ceiling and interact with the powerbox to get the power back on.

- Go back to the main room and click on the mold on the wall.
